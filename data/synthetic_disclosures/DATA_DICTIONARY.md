# Data dictionary

- `record_id`: unique synthetic scenario ID.
- `company`, `industry`, `fiscal_year`: fictional entity and period metadata.
- `risk_category`: illustrative risk category.
- `claim_text`: claim to inspect.
- `evidence_context`: constructed supporting-source context.
- `source_type`: source label used in scenario.
- `S_staleness`, `C_conflict`, `M_missingness`, `A_low_authority`: binary defect indicators (1 = present).
- `materiality`: illustrative analyst-assessed materiality.
- `illustrative_reference_label`: scenario label, not verified ground truth.
