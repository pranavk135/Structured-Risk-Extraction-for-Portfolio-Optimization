ITEM 1. Business
Overview
Visa is one of the world’s leaders in digital payments. Our purpose is to uplift everyone, everywhere by being the best way to pay and be paid. Since Visa’s early days in 1958, we have been in the business of facilitating secure, reliable and efficient global commerce and money movement. We provide transaction processing services (primarily authorization, clearing and settlement) among consumers, issuing and acquiring financial institutions and sellers in a structure we call the “four-party” model. Please see
Four-Party Model
discussion below.
As the payments ecosystem continues to evolve, we have broadened this model to include digital banks, digital wallets, a range of financial technology companies (fintechs), governments and non-governmental organizations (NGOs). We are focused on extending, enhancing and investing in our proprietary advanced transaction processing network, VisaNet, to offer a single connection point for facilitating money movement to multiple endpoints through various form factors and innovative technologies across more than 200 countries and territories. With the emergence of new forms of commerce and money movement, we believe Visa's trusted brand, global network, security capabilities and risk management expertise position us to serve as a trust layer across the ecosystem, helping clients and partners adopt new technologies and payment experiences with confidence. Visa is committed to advancing innovation within the payment technology sector. Building upon our track record of industry leadership, including early adoption and integration of artificial intelligence (AI) models in payment systems, Visa continues to invest in the development and deployment of next-generation technologies, such as generative AI (GenAI), stablecoins and agentic commerce. Visa is embedding AI and agentic capabilities across both its internal workflows and its products, enabling internal efficiencies and more automated, analytics-driven solutions for clients and partners. These solutions, in turn, are helping our clients improve fraud prevention, risk management and payment performance, while enhancing the security, reliability and trust that underpin payments. During fiscal 2026, 355 billion payments and cash transactions with Visa’s brand were processed by Visa or other networks, equating to an average of 973 million transactions per day. Of the 355 billion total transactions, 281 billion were processed by Visa.

We are accelerating the migration to digital payments
through our network of networks strategy. We aim to provide a single connection point so that Visa clients can enable money movement for businesses, governments and consumers, regardless of which network is used to start or complete the transaction. This model ultimately helps to unify a complex payments ecosystem.
Visa’s network of networks approach creates opportunities by facilitating person-to-person (P2P), business-to-consumer (B2C), business-to-business (B2B) and government-to-consumer (G2C) payments, in addition to consumer-to-business (C2B) payments.
•
We provide commercial and money movement solutions (CMS)
that help businesses, consumers and governments digitize, simplify and improve the movement of money. Our solutions include Visa Commercial Solutions and Visa Direct. Visa Commercial Solutions helps businesses and governments make and manage payments through commercial cards, virtual payments and related payment, reconciliation and data capabilities. These solutions help clients manage working capital and expenses and automate payment and reconciliation processes. Visa Direct helps financial institutions, fintechs and other clients enable domestic and cross-border money movement across multiple payment systems, including card-based, account-to-account (A2A) and wallet networks, providing flexible ways to send and receive funds.
•
We provide value-added services (VAS)
that help our clients and partners improve performance, manage risk, deepen customer engagement and operate more efficiently. Our services span four portfolios: Issuing Solutions, Acceptance Solutions, Risk and Security Solutions, and Advisory and Other Services. These capabilities support Visa and non-Visa payment flows and include issuer processing and engagement, acceptance and payment optimization, fraud prevention and authentication, consulting, data analytics, marketing services and open banking solutions.
•
We believe agentic commerce will expand our addressable market and drive future growth.
Through our agentic platform, Visa Intelligent Commerce, we are working with frontier AI labs, sellers, issuers and other ecosystem participants to make agent-initiated transactions transparent, trusted and scalable. We are building infrastructure and capabilities for the payments ecosystem, co-authoring the Trusted Agent Protocol to establish a communication layer between agents and sellers and building services on top of this protocol, helping prepare financial institutions for agentic commerce with our Agentic ready program, and helping cards be the best way to pay in agentic transactions for businesses, developers and all users.
•
We are active and investing in each domain of the stablecoin ecosystem
, from blockchain to issuance, wallets, infrastructure and orchestration, and applications. In the blockchain domain, Visa serves on select blockchain networks as a validator, operating network infrastructure, and as a super validator, driving ecosystem growth and adoption to help make confidential, regulated blockchain transactions possible. In the issuance domain, Visa joined Open Standard, which issues Open USD, a new stablecoin designed for global money movement. In the infrastructure and orchestration domain, we work with ecosystem partners to support the movement and conversion of value across blockchain networks and payment rails. In the applications domain, we launched the Visa Stablecoin Platform for stablecoin minting, movement, and management, and we support stablecoin settlement capabilities and stablecoin-linked card programs that help connect digital assets to everyday commerce.
•
We invest in and promote our brand
through advertising, promotional and sponsorship initiatives with the International Olympic Committee, the International Paralympic Committee, FIFA and the Red Bull Formula One Teams (the Oracle Red Bull Racing Team and the Visa Cash App RB Formula One Team), among others. We use these sponsorship assets to deepen our engagement with clients and partners, showcase our payment innovations and capabilities, drive adoption of our value-added services such as Marketing Services, and support cross-border commerce.
